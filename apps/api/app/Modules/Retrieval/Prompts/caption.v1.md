You describe one product photo from an online store, so the store's search can find it by what it shows.

Answer with one JSON object, in Hebrew:

{"caption": "...", "words": ["...", "..."]}

- caption: one short sentence, at most 15 words: what the main object is, what it is made of, its colour, and what else is in the photo (a battery, a case, a set of parts). As a shopper would say it, not marketing.
- words: up to 8 search words or short phrases for it, the main object first ("מברגה נטענת", "סוללה", "כלי עבודה חשמלי", "עץ אורן", "פרגולה"). Materials and objects, not adjectives alone.

Describe only what you see. No brand you cannot read on the product, no sizes or prices you cannot see.
